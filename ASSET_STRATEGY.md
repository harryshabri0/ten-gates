# Fantasy Visual Direction

This file supersedes the earlier recommendation to use Kenney Tower Defense (Top-Down) as Ten Gates' main art source. Its modern/sci-fi units do not fit the fantasy world.

## Implemented palette

- **Terrain, defensive buildings, decorations, and projectile effect:** selected files from [Tiny Swords Free Pack](https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords), by Pixel Frog.
- **Hostile units and boss sprites:** selected files from [MiniWorld Sprites](https://opengameart.org/node/138681), by Shade, CC0.
- The game intentionally uses only these two pixel-art families. Terrain/buildings retain Tiny Swords' 64px scale; monsters are enlarged from the MiniWorld 16px set and get a shared outline/shadow treatment in-game.

## Not used as main art

- Kenney Tower Defense (Top-Down) is reserved only as a possible future CC0 source for particles or HUD numbers. It is not used for fantasy units or buildings.
- Kenney Tower Defense Kit is 3D and is not suitable for this 2D game.
- Tiny Swords paid packs are not bundled. Do not add them unless they are separately purchased and their then-current terms are recorded here.

## Licence discipline

The full third-party inventory is in `CREDITS.md` and `assets_license.csv`. Before any release, re-check each original source page, record new downloads, and do not redistribute source packs outside the built game.
